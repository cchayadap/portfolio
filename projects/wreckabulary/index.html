The problem

Word games usually mean sitting still and staring at tiles, while party brawlers rarely ask you to think. Wreckabulary asks what happens when spelling is the weapon: in a cozy world where every object is built from its own letters, smashing the room hands you the vocabulary to fight back.

How it works

  • Smash
  • Scavenge
  • Spell
  • Summon

Smash furniture and it bursts into the letters that spell it. Walk over letters to carry up to six, then spell a word letter by letter over your head. Real words are cast instantly: action words like BLADE, WINGS or BEES do something, and object words build the thing Word World-style, like a SOFA with S and A armrests. Getting hit knocks letters loose, so everything you summon can be smashed and re-spelled.

Everything starts in the hub, a house friends join by walking in the front door. A typewriter picks the mode: Dibs! (versus), Furnish First (versus), Moving Day (co-op) or Home Sweet Home (a creative sandbox with rooms saved as JSON).
