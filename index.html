About me

Chompoo

Hi, I'm Chayada, though most people call me Chompoo! Most of what I do lands somewhere between "full-stack developer" and "person who wants to make a game one day." I've always cared about society and what people actually need, so during my studies I've contributed to projects in healthcare and student communities. In this era of AI beating mathematicians and artists at their own craft, I've come to believe what we need now is human hands and creativity! I want to work with people and keep improving my skills, so I can help bring the fun back to our tech industry, in the most effective way I can <3

  • Tinkering in Unity and Godot on the side
  • Once taught a simulated stick insect to walk
  • Saving the world from AI with our creativity
  • Powered by books and browser tabs
